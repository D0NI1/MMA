About the Kosovo MMA Federation

The **Kosovo MMA Federation (KMMAF)** is the official governing body for amateur and professional Mixed Martial Arts in the Republic of Kosovo. Established to promote, regulate, and safeguard the sport, KMMAF is dedicated to fostering talent, ensuring fair play, and upholding the highest standards of athletic integrity and safety.

🎯 Mission Statement

To cultivate a robust, ethical, and internationally competitive MMA landscape in Kosovo, providing clear pathways for athletes and officials to achieve excellence while prioritizing safety and compliance with global sporting standards.

Licensing and Certification

Athlete Registration

All competitors must hold an annual KMMAF Athlete License. This includes:

  • Proof of Medical Clearance
  • WADA Anti-Doping Education Completion
  • Affiliation with a certified KMMAF Club
Apply for Athlete License

Official Certification

We host regular courses for individuals looking to become certified Referees or Judges under IMMAF guidelines.

  • KMMAF Level 1 Officiating Course (Upcoming: March 2026)
  • Continuous Education Seminars
Register for Course

Anti-Doping Policy

The KMMAF strictly adheres to the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) code. All registered athletes are subject to testing. Integrity and fair competition are paramount to our sport.
